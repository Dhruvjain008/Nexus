## The Problem

Useful equipment and specialised spaces often sit idle in institutions, labs, and workshops—while students, researchers, and creators struggle to access them. Unclear availability, eligibility requirements, competing bookings, and disputes over equipment condition make resource sharing difficult.

## The NEXUS Solution

**NEXUS turns resource sharing into a structured, transparent workflow—from discovery and booking to handover and accountability.**

Instead of treating a resource as just a listing, NEXUS brings borrowers, resource owners, and managers into one experience designed to improve utilisation, fair access, and trust.

## Key Features

- **Resource Discovery** — Explore equipment and spaces with details, availability, and access requirements.
- **Eligibility-Aware Booking** — Surface training or qualification requirements before access.
- **Owner Approvals** — Give resource owners a dedicated workflow to review booking requests.
- **FairShare Prioritisation** — A usage-aware approach to help reduce resource monopolisation when demand competes.
- **Check-in & Check-out** — Record handover details and before/after condition checks.
- **Incident Reporting** — Document damage, delays, or other issues for review.
- **Role-Based Workspaces** — Separate views for borrowers, resource owners, managers, and administrators.
- **Audit & Oversight** — Make important actions and decisions easier to review.

## What Makes NEXUS Different?

NEXUS focuses on the **whole lifecycle of shared resources**, not just listing them. Fair-use prioritisation addresses the challenge of repeated access by the same users; condition records and incident workflows help create accountability; and role-specific views make responsibilities clearer.

The goal is simple: **make existing resources more discoverable, more fairly accessible, and easier to share responsibly.**

## Hackathon Impact

NEXUS addresses the challenge of underused equipment and spaces by connecting **discovery, eligibility, booking decisions, fair-use principles, handover records, and incident review** in one platform. The approach can be adapted to laboratories, makerspaces, research facilities, computing resources, and shared institutional spaces.

## Trust & Responsible Use

Eligibility indicators and condition reports can support better decisions, but they do not independently prove credential authenticity or determine liability for damage. Important approvals and disputes should remain reviewable by responsible people. Production use would also require verification of authentication, permissions, persistence, privacy, and security in the deployed implementation.
